A note for grown-ups

Word Zap helps Nivi recognize familiar words on sight — not just sound them out letter by letter.

  • Listen rounds: the game says a word. He taps the truck that shows that word.
  • Read-it-yourself rounds: the word appears in big letters (no auto audio). He finds the same word on a truck. The speaker button will still read it if he wants help.
  • A wrong tap just wobbles. There is no penalty and no timer. He can try again.
  • 10 rounds, about 5 minutes. Each star is a word he found. At the end he unlocks a new vehicle for his garage (saved on this iPad).

This week's words: pig, fox, sun, mud, van, run, sled, flip, plus we, I, you, my.

Tip: celebrate a quick tap — that's a ZAP — when he doesn't need to sound the word out.
